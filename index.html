Scope of this tool. Implements Section 7 (Design Traffic / ESA calculation), the Section 11.8.3 pavement catalogue for granular pavements with thin bituminous surfacing (design traffic ≤ 3×106 ESA — covers the great majority of provincial, collector and local-access roads), and the Section 9 minimum-requirement tables for rigid (concrete) pavements. It does not implement the full mechanistic-empirical procedure of Section 8 / 9.4 (layered elastic analysis, fatigue and erosion nomographs) — that requires specialist software and a qualified pavement engineer. Every figure below is transcribed from the source PDF and cited by clause; verify against the original document for final design submissions.
